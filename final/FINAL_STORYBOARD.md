## The one-sentence version

Two people meet on the sideline of a lacrosse field, spend eleven years being
just each other, become five, get completely overrun by the life they built —
and at the end, when the noise drops out, they are still the two people from the
first frame.

---

## What the archive let us do — and what it didn't

Phase 1 found that **77 of 98 couple-alone photographs predate the first
daughter**. In this cut that finding *is* the structure rather than a problem to
patch:

| | Ch 1–2 (before children) | Ch 3–6 (the family years) | Ch 7 (the return) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Couple-alone frames | 32 | 3 | 8 |

The density of "just us" pictures collapses on screen exactly the way it
collapses in the archive, and then deliberately comes back. Nothing on screen
explains this. It is felt, not stated.

**The wedding is represented by one text card and the house they bought.** No
ceremony photographs are used, needed, or referenced beyond the date.

### Two honest flags

1. **The film opens in 2000, not 2002.** The earliest hard-dated frames — her in
   her lacrosse uniform, him on the sideline; the two of them at a school formal
   — carry burned-in print stamps from spring 2000, two years before the verified
   March 15, 2002 start of the relationship. The cut uses this on purpose: he was
   already there. The "March 15, 2002." card lands *after* the opening frame, so
   it reads as the day it became official rather than the day they met. If you'd
   rather the film be strictly literal, see *Opening variants* in
   `CANVA_BUILD_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
2. **The three daughters are represented by era and age, not by name.** Phase 1
   never identified them individually and this cut does not guess. Coverage is
   built from the birth order you verified — first (Oct 30, 2013), second
   (Nov 1, 2016), third (Sept 2020) — so each daughter's arrival, first birthday
   and recent life are all present. **You should sanity-check that each girl feels
   fairly seen before you export.** That is the one judgement I cannot make from
   the pixels.

### CHAPTER 7 — STILL US · 11 photographs · 7:18–8:47

with the youngest in the MAMA beanie. Then, at 7:31, the rarest thing in the

changes subject.

Then the two of you, and only the two of you, for the rest of the film — with

2017, 2022, 2023, the balcony.

Card: **But one thing hasn't.**

Then twelve seconds on the final frame, VO-12 over it, and three cards.

not guess. Before you export, watch Chapters 4–6 once and check that each of the
three feels fairly seen. If one is thin, tell me and I will rebalance — the
substitutions are already ranked in the Phase 1 best-of files.
