## 4. Dates

| Confidence | Count |
|---|---:|
| HIGH (EXIF or a burned-in date stamp) | 253 |
| MEDIUM (visually dated, era certain) | 61 |
| LOW (visually dated, range only) | 96 |
| UNKNOWN | 4 |

**Date range represented: spring 2000 → April 2026.**

Two dating traps were found and handled:

1. **All 110 scans carry EXIF timestamps of the day they were scanned**, because
   Google PhotoScan stamps the scan time. Trusted at face value, the entire
   2003–2013 archive would have been filed under "TODAY" and the film's
   chronology inverted. These are detected by their `Software` tag and dated
   visually instead.
2. **64 digital photographs have their EXIF stripped**, so they sorted to the
   end of the sequence rather than into their real place. They were dated by
   counting the children visible in frame against the three known birth years —
   a reliable bracket.

**Burned-in camera date stamps** on the prints are the only hard dates before
2013. Readable ones: `00. 4.23`, `00. 5.10`, `00. 5.14` (spring 2000);
`03.17.2007`, `05.11.2007`, `05.19.2007`, `05.25.2007`, `05.27.2007`,
`07.04.2007`, `08.05.2007`. A second dating axis is his hair — full hair in the
earliest frames, buzzed around 2008–2010, fully shaved thereafter.

**77 of 98 couple photographs predate your first daughter.** One photograph of
the two of you in three years, then two in the following four.

This is both the film's biggest practical problem — the ending is supposed to
land on the strongest recent photograph of the two of you, and the candidates
are few — and its most honest piece of evidence. The density of couple
photographs falling off a cliff exactly when the children arrive is the visual
proof of the voiceover's "years where we were just trying to keep up."

## 12. Technical limitations encountered

- **Google Drive is reachable only through the connector**, one file per call —
  direct HTTP is blocked by this environment's network policy. 414 files took
  ~450 individual calls.
- **The connector rate-limits intermittently**, returning an approval error
  after roughly 8 consecutive downloads. It clears on its own; retry passes
  recovered every file. No photograph was permanently lost.
- **Drive IDs may contain or end with `_`**, which silently corrupted
  file-identity checks until caught. Fixed with exact prefix matching. Worth
  knowing for Phase 2.
- **Concurrent downloads can collide** on the saved-result filename and lose a
  payload. Phase 2 should download serially per worker.
- **Folder 02's files were re-uploaded mid-session with new Drive IDs**,
  invalidating 7 earlier downloads. Detected and re-fetched.
- **Face recognition was not used.** People are identified by role, era, and
  count, never by name, and the daughters are not individually identified.
