# 15 YEARS OF US — PRELIMINARY STORYBOARD

**Soundtrack:** "Just Breathe" — Pearl Jam
**Voiceover:** recorded by you, not generated.
**Status:** PRELIMINARY. Not a final cut, not a final selection, not a final count.

This storyboard describes what the **current** archive could contribute to each
chapter. Chapter 2 is currently unmakeable — see the gap note in that section.

---

## THE ARGUMENT

The film should not say "here is our life." It should say one thing:

> We grew up together, we built a family, we survived the chaos —
> and after all of it, I would still choose you.

Everything serves that. The children are the evidence, not the subject. The film
opens on the couple and ends on the couple, and the middle earns the ending.

**The structural discovery that should shape the edit:** 77 of the 98
couple-alone photographs in this archive were taken before your first daughter
was born. After 2013 the two of you nearly vanish from your own record. That is
not a flaw to hide — it is the film's central movement made visible. The frame
fills up with children and the two of you get pushed to the edges, and then at
the end the film deliberately clears the frame and puts you back in it.

## PACING

| Type | Duration |
|---|---|
| Hero photograph | 4–7s |
| Normal photograph | 2.5–4s |
| Montage run | 1.5–2.5s |
| Extremely meaningful | 6–10s |
| Final frame | 8–10s |

Let it breathe. Do not cut to the beat. "Just Breathe" is a quiet song and the
edit should sit inside it, not ride it.

At roughly 90–110 photographs and these durations, expect **5 to 6 minutes** —
longer than the song, which means either a second music bed under the later
chapters or an instrumental reprise. Flagging it now because it affects how many
photographs survive the final cut.

## CHAPTER 1 — BEFORE WE KNEW
*Opening. Roughly 12–16 photographs.*

**On screen:**
> BEFORE THE HOUSE.
> BEFORE THE KIDS.
> BEFORE THE SCHEDULES.
> BEFORE WE KNEW WHAT LIFE WOULD BECOME.
> THERE WAS JUST US.

**What the archive gives us:** 63 photographs, 48 of them the two of you alone.
This chapter is far stronger than expected.

**Opening image — recommendation:** `S037`. He has swept her off her feet
mid-laugh in a student room, CRT television and taped-up posters behind them.
It is unguarded, unmistakably young, and it looks nothing like the rest of the
film. Open there and the audience knows immediately this is not a slideshow.

**Then, roughly in order:**
- `S061` — one of the earliest frames of the two of them, dark and grainy. Let
  it be imperfect; that is the point.
- `S003` — the prom portrait, hard-dated by a burned-in stamp to spring 2000.
- `S026` — a kiss at the edge of a party, no audience. Hold this one.
- `S028` — the laughing companion frame to it.
- `S029` — a wine toast in a borrowed cabin, one of their first trips away.
- `S076` — unposed and laughing in the back seat of a car.
- `S042`, `S043` — the college years exactly as they were: plastic cups, taped
  photo walls, a kiss on the cheek.
- `S062`, `S063` — an early trip south, Caribbean water.
- `S077` — one of her birthdays during the dating years.
- `S036` — the two of them in front of a house they clearly wanted
  photographed. This is the "before" that sets up everything after.

**Open question that affects this chapter:** date stamps put the earliest
photographs in spring **2000**, three years before the "together since 2003"
framing. If you go back further than the film currently says, the closing text
needs a different number.

## CHAPTER 2 — THE DAY WE SAID I DO
*Roughly 10–14 photographs. **Currently unmakeable.***

**On screen:**
> THEN WE SAID
> I DO.
> AND SOMEHOW...
> THAT WAS ALREADY 15 YEARS AGO.

### ⚠ There are zero photographs of your wedding in this archive.

Not one. No gown, no veil, no tuxedo, no ceremony, no reception, no first dance.
Two independent reviews of all 110 scanned prints found the same thing. The only
weddings present are other people's, where she is a bridesmaid (`S106`) and he
is a groomsman (`S091`).

This is the chapter the anniversary is *for*, and it is empty.

**This is the top item in `PHOTO_HUNT.md`.** Your parents, her parents, the
bridal party, or the original photographer are the most likely sources.

**If the photographs genuinely cannot be found,** the honest fallback is to
build the chapter from what a wedding meant rather than what it looked like:
`S098` (the most formal portrait of the two of you in the archive) held long
under the "I DO" text, then straight into early marriage. It is a compromise and
it will read as one. Finding the album is worth real effort first.

them the couple alone, with an unusually well-documented 2007.

**Suggested spine:**

- `S065` — hard-dated `07.04.2007`.
- `S083` — a snowstorm, both laughing, snow visibly on his head. **Hero.**
- `S101` — the two of them laughing in turquoise ocean. **Hero.**

**Ending this chapter:** hold on a couple frame a beat longer than comfortable,

## CHAPTER 4 — THEN THERE WERE FIVE
*Roughly 18–22 photographs. The archive's strongest chapter.*

**On screen:**
> ONE BECAME TWO.
> THEN THREE.
> THEN FOUR.
> THEN FIVE.
> And somehow... This became our life.

All three arrivals are documented, which is rare and lucky.

**The sequence:**
1. `D001` (2013-11-01) — hospital, newborn in both your arms. **Hero, 6–8s.**
   This is the hinge of the entire film.
2. `D004` — the toddler's face filling the frame, dad grinning underneath.
3. `D006` — all three of you laughing, tight crop.
4. `D013` (2016-11-01) — the second birth. **Same framing as `D001`, three
   years later to the day.** Cut them close together and let the rhyme land.
5. `D014` — black and white, hospital bed, all four together for the first
   time. **The strongest emotional photograph in the archive. Hero, 8s.**
6. `D015` — the older sister meeting the baby, shot from behind her.
7. `D016` — dad holding the newborn while the big sister holds her doll in the
   identical pose.
8. `D085` (2020-08-18) — the visibly pregnant beach portrait, four about to be
   five.
9. `D241` (~Sept 2020) — the going-home frame: bow-wrapped newborn in the car
   seat, **both parents in face masks**. **Hero.** It dates itself and carries
   the era in one image.
10. `D087` (2020-10-31) — shot from directly overhead, all four laid out in a
    row on the rug, the newborn's first Halloween.

**Confirm for me:** are both older daughters really born on November 1st? If so
the `D001` → `D013` cut is the best structural gift in the archive.

**Weak seam:** only 10 photographs exist for 2013–2016. The chapter's middle is
thin and `PHOTO_HUNT.md` targets it.

## CHAPTER 5 — THE CHAOS
*Roughly 12–16 photographs. Faster cutting, 1.5–2.5s, then one long hold.*

**On screen:**
> IT WASN'T ALWAYS PERFECT.
> ACTUALLY... IT WAS RARELY PERFECT.
> BUT IT WAS OURS.
> And I wouldn't trade it.

Run these fast and let them be ugly:
- `D019` — the double stroller and the coffee.
- `D010` — both of you dressed formally, a small daughter photobombing at knee
  height.
- `D020` — dad sprawled in a lawn chair, daughter beside him in a tiny chair
  copying his exact pose.
- `D180` — the girls covering his shaved head in gem stickers. **Unrepeatable.**
- `D260` — masks at the Rockefeller tree, Dec 2021.
- Backyard inflatable pool, trail walks, matching pyjamas.

**Then stop the montage dead and hold on `D066` (2020-03-30), 8 seconds.**
The girls' hands pressed flat against a glass storm door, an older grey-haired
man kept outside on the walk. It is the most historically eloquent frame in the
archive and it earns silence. Put it under "BUT IT WAS OURS."

## CHAPTER 6 — THE LIFE WE BUILT
*Roughly 16–20 photographs. **Cut this chapter hardest.***

**On screen:**
> 15 YEARS OF MEMORIES.
> 15 YEARS OF LAUGHTER.
> 15 YEARS OF CHAOS.
> 15 YEARS OF GROWING.
> 15 YEARS OF US.

This chapter has more strong material than Chapters 1–4 combined and is the
least important to the argument. Discipline here is what keeps the film from
becoming a slideshow.

**The best of it:**
- `D122` (2023-06-18) — all three girls draped over their father while he
  reads. **Hero.** The whole family-of-five in one frame.
- `D131` (2023-07-31) — dusk, backs turned, carrying one daughter and leading
  another.
- `D138` (2023-08-03) — three sisters tangled on empty sand at last light.
  **Hero.**
- `D107` (2022-08-03) — arms up in triumph, a daughter clamped to each side.
- `D113` — four faces stacked at different depths, none of them arranged.
- The lacrosse thread — `D216`/`D217`, the three sisters from behind in
  identical family-name jerseys, tallest to smallest. **Hero.**
- One Christmas frame. **One.** The archive has many and they all say the same
  thing.

**A cut worth building deliberately:** one scanned print shows a women's
lacrosse game around 2000 (`S006`/`S014`). If that player is her, cut from it
straight to `D216` — the same sport, twenty-five years apart, her then and her
daughters now. **Confirm whether that's her.** If it is, it's the single most
authored moment available to this film.

## CHAPTER 7 — TODAY
*Roughly 8–12 photographs, slowing throughout.*

**On screen:**
> 15 YEARS MARRIED.
> 23 YEARS OF US.
> A LOT HAS CHANGED.
> BUT ONE THING HASN'T.
> I'D STILL CHOOSE YOU.

- `D263` (2022) — all five on the playroom floor in holiday pyjamas, every face
  genuinely lit up. The best family-of-five photograph you have.
- `D283` — four faces filling the frame, all laughing.
- `D216` — the three sisters walking out to the field.

Then clear the children out of the frame and end on the two of you.

**Final frame — recommendation:** `D300`. Formally dressed on a deck over a
river valley, just the two of you, visibly older than where the film started.
It rhymes with `S037` at the opening — the same two people, twenty-odd years
apart, still leaning into each other.

> HAPPY 15TH ANNIVERSARY MY LOVE.
> 15 YEARS DOWN. FOREVER TO GO.

Hold 8–10 seconds. Fade to black.

**Alternates:** `D111` (warm low sun, treeline gone gold — the most unguarded
recent frame) or `D018` (2017, sunglasses, cheek to cheek — the best of the
middle years, but eight years old).

## WHAT MUST BE SETTLED BEFORE THE FINAL CUT

1. **The wedding photographs.** Chapter 2 does not exist without them.
2. **Is the Atlantis trip the honeymoon?** If yes, Chapter 2 partially recovers.
3. **Are both older daughters born on November 1st?**
4. **Is the ~2000 lacrosse player her?**
5. **Do you actually go back to 2000, not 2003?** The closing text says 23
   years; the date stamps suggest more.
6. **Your own pick for the most beautiful photograph of her.** An archivist
   can't choose that one, and the film ends on it.
